- DepositPointsOutcome: affectedPointAccountTypeIds[] = bucket being credited (Escrow hold, Spendable earn, or tier-qual counter).

- Never invent pointAccountTypeId — use PointAccountManifest items[] / GetPointAccountType / upsert tool results.
- After PAT upserts, confirm manifest roles: spendable, tierQualification, escrow.
- Expiration on Spendable does not change tier-qual balance unless you also deposit to the tier-qual PAT.
- expiresToPointAccountTypeId on a PAT is for engine cascade — not referenced in journey JSON.
- Full PAT semantics (expiration, cascade, golden bundles): loaded during Point Account Types phase — do not invent ledgerType TierQualification.
